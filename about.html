Our Story

From Corner Shop to Community Favourite

FreshMart started in 2015 as a small family-owned grocery store with one simple goal: make fresh, quality food accessible to every household in our neighbourhood.

In 2018, we launched same-day delivery — and everything changed. Families who couldn't make it to the store could suddenly get farm-fresh produce at their door. We grew from 200 orders a month to 5,000+ a week.

Today, we're still family-owned. Still community-focused. And still obsessing over every apple, every loaf, and every doorstep we deliver to.

Our Journey

Ten Years, One Mission

A decade of serving our community, one delivery at a time.

Year 2015

The Corner Shop Opens

FreshMart opens its doors as a tiny 400 sq ft family store on Market Street — just one apple crate and a big idea.

Year 2017

First Partner Farms

We partner with 5 local farms to source fresher, more sustainable produce. Our farm-to-door promise begins.

Year 2018

Delivery Launches

Same-day delivery launches. We hit 200 orders in the first month — and never looked back.

Year 2020

Community Support

During lockdown, we delivered 50,000 free grocery boxes to vulnerable households across our city.

Year 2022

40 Partner Farms

Our farm network grows to 40+ local growers — all within 50 miles of our store.

Today 2025

5,000 Households

We now serve over 5,000 families every week — and we're just getting started.
