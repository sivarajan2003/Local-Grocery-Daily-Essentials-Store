Organic FAQs

Questions About Organic

Everything you need to know about our organic range.

What does certified organic mean?

Grown without synthetic pesticides, GMOs, or artificial fertilisers, verified by USDA and EU standards.

Are all your organic products local?

Most organic produce comes from local farms within 50 miles. Pantry items come from certified organic suppliers.

Is organic more expensive?

Organic costs slightly more because it's grown slowly and sustainably. We work hard to keep our prices fair.

Do organic products ship same-day?

Yes — all organic items qualify for same-day delivery in our service zones. Free over $30.

Are organic products guaranteed fresh?

Absolutely. Every organic product is backed by our freshness promise — full refund if not satisfied.

How do I know a product is organic?

Look for the green "ORGANIC" badge on the product card and the certification logo on the product page.
