About FreshMart

FreshMart started as a small family-owned store in 2015 with one simple goal: make fresh, quality groceries accessible to every household in our community.

A decade later, we serve over 5,000 families with same-day delivery, locally-sourced produce, and a curated pantry selection. We're still family-owned, still community-focused, and still obsessing over every apple and every loaf.
